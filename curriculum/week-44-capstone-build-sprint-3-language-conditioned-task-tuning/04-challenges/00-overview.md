# Week 44 — Challenges

The exercises build the apparatus. **The challenge is the capstone bar made real.** It takes the rest of the week's hours and produces the single most important artifact for your week-48 defense: a policy that clears 15/20, plus an honest accounting of every instruction it does not.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Drive per-instruction success to ≥ 15/20](./challenge-01-drive-to-fifteen-of-twenty.md)** — get at least fifteen of twenty instructions to pass the frozen suite, and for every still-failing instruction document the specific failure mode and the next concrete data or scaffolding change that would fix it. (~4-6 h across Thursday–Saturday)

This challenge is **not optional** for the capstone. The week-48 panel grades your policy against exactly this bar. If you skip the challenge, you arrive at the defense without knowing your number — which is the one thing the defense exists to test. Do it.

The deliverable is two things: the number (≥ 15/20, honestly measured) and the diagnosis (mode + next fix for each remaining failure). The second is what turns a brittle "it passed" into a senior "here is exactly where it stands and what I'd do next" — the answer that lands in week 45's mock interviews and the week-48 defense alike.
