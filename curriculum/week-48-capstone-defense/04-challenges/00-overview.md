# Week 48 — Challenges

One challenge, and it is the whole point of the year: the full mock defense, run against the real rubric before the real defense, so you find and close the gaps while they're cheap. This is the dress rehearsal for the panel that decides whether you graduate.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — The full mock defense](./challenge-01-the-full-mock-defense.md)** — run the complete ninety-minute defense (opening pitch → stack walkthrough → videos → acceptance-criteria mapping → safety case → chaos drills → live Q&A) against the real rubric, with a mock panel, and turn its findings into a gap-closing sprint before Saturday. (~3 hours including debrief)

## How to work the challenge

- **Run the whole ninety minutes**, not a casual run-through. The timing and the Q&A pressure are the point (Lecture 1 §6).
- **Bring the complete package** (Exercise 1) and the measured acceptance table (Exercise 2). You can't defend what you haven't assembled and measured.
- **Drill the Q&A bank** (Exercise 3) before the mock; the panel digs three layers and expects a number.
- **Treat it as real.** A mock with a soft panel and softball questions teaches nothing and sets you up to find the gaps live (Lecture 1 §6).
- **Surface every gap honestly.** An honest gap with a plan beats a hidden one; an unaddressed safety defect is the one unforgivable fail (Lecture 1 §4).
