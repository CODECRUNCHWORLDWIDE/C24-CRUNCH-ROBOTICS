# Week 9 — Challenges

The exercises drill the mechanics. **The challenge makes you prove the week's headline claim with a number** — that calibration measurably reduces integrated drift — so "calibration matters" stops being a slogan and becomes a factor you measured on your own sensor.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Quantify the yaw-drift reduction](challenge-01-quantify-yaw-drift-reduction.md)** — integrate yaw from raw vs. bias-corrected gyro over the same stationary window, plot both, and report the drift-reduction factor. (~90 min)

Challenges are optional for passing the week, but this one produces *the* artifact for the Phase 2 midterm: a before/after drift plot with a hard number. When a reviewer asks "how do you know your IMU calibration works?", the answer is this plot. Do it.
