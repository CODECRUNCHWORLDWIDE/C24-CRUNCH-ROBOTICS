# Week 31 — Challenges

The exercises get your data and your tokenizer ready. **The challenge is the result the whole week exists to produce:** a zero-shot-vs-fine-tuned A/B on your own task, with a failure analysis honest enough to survive the Week 32 midterm panel.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Zero-shot vs. fine-tuned, with a failure analysis](./challenge-01-zero-shot-vs-finetuned.md)** — fine-tune OpenVLA on your Week 29 data, evaluate it and the zero-shot baseline on a held-out set, report the honest number, and classify every failure into perception / grounding / control. (~3–4 h, including one cloud-GPU fine-tune)

Challenges are optional for passing the week, but this one is the single best preparation for the **Phase 4 second midterm in Week 32**, where you defend a learned policy — quite possibly this exact checkpoint — to a panel. The skill it builds — running an honest A/B and diagnosing *why* a learned policy fails rather than waving at "it's not great yet" — is exactly what separates an engineer who *deploys* a VLA from one who only *demos* one. Do it.
