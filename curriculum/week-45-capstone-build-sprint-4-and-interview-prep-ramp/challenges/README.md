# Week 45 — Challenges

One challenge this week, and it is the hardest kind: open-ended, adversarial, and about your *own* work. There is no test harness. The grader is a human trying to find the hole in your reasoning.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Defend your stack through three layers of "why"](challenge-01-defend-your-stack.md)** — pick one real design decision from your capstone and survive recursive "why" questioning down to the third layer without collapsing into "it's what the tutorial used."

## Why this is the challenge

Every other skill this week — the box diagram, the EKF math, the STAR story — is *preparation* for one moment: the interviewer picks the one decision you're least sure about and asks "why?" four times. The candidate who can hold up through that is the candidate who actually understands their robot. The candidate who can't gets exposed as someone who assembled a tutorial.

This challenge trains exactly that moment, on your real capstone, with a real human pushing back. Do not skip it. It is the single highest-leverage thing in the week.
