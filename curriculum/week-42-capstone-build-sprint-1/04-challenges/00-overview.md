# Week 42 — Challenges

The exercises get the stack onto hardware (or into a hardened deployment) and produce a first number. **The challenge makes that number meet the capstone bar.** It takes 2–3 hours and produces the single most important artifact of the week — the one the Week 48 panel will scrutinize.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — The half-meter drift bar](./challenge-01-half-meter-drift-bar.md)** *(~2.5 h)* — Demonstrate that the fused state estimate drifts **under 0.5 m over a 20-meter trajectory**, on hardware (Path A) or in the hardened sim deployment (Path B). This is the capstone acceptance bar, three sprints early. Produces a recorded run, a drift plot, and a defensible number.

The challenge is **not optional** if you intend to pass the Week 48 capstone defense. The whole point of Week 42 is to find out how far you are from 0.5 m while you still have six weeks to close the gap. Hit it now and the rest of the capstone is polish; miss it now and you know exactly what to fix in Weeks 43–47.
