# Week 25 — Challenges

The exercises drill the geometry. **The challenge makes you the manipulation engineer who has to defend a grasp.** You're handed three different objects and asked not just to grasp them, but to explain — for each — why the grasp your planner picked is the right one, and what would make it fail.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Grasp three objects, and defend the top grasp](challenge-01-grasp-three-objects.md)** — run your antipodal planner on three different objects (a cylinder/can, a box, and a tool or mug), rank the candidates, visualize the top grasps, and write a defense of why each object's top grasp is the top grasp — and what geometry would make it fail. (~90 min)

Challenges are optional for passing the week, but this one is the single best preparation for Week 26, where you deploy Contact-GraspNet and compare its grasps against your heuristic's — and you can only say "the network's grasp is better/worse than mine" if you can defend what *yours* picked and why. The skill — looking at a ranked grasp set and naming, in geometric terms, why one grasp beats another — is exactly what separates an engineer who runs a grasp planner from one who can debug why it dropped the cup.
