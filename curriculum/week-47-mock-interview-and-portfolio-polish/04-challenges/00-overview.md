# Week 47 — Challenges

One challenge this week: the full-loop mock interview. Five rounds, back to back, with a senior reviewer whose job is to find the gap between your portfolio's claims and your actual knowledge. This is the syllabus's mock robotics-startup interview (5% of the track) and the dress rehearsal for the Week 48 defense.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — The full-loop mock](./challenge-01-full-loop-mock.md)** — run the complete five-round loop (intro → technical deep-dive → system design → behavioral/portfolio → founder/culture) with a senior reviewer, survive the deep dive, and get scored and debriefed against a rubric. (~3 hours including debrief)

## How to work the challenge

- **Bring your artifacts.** Latency report, eval-suite results, chaos-drill postmortems, architecture diagram. "Here's the panel" beats "I recall it was fast" (Lecture 1 §7).
- **Run the seven-phase method** in the system-design round, watching the clock (Lecture 1 §3) — pacing is the most common weak round.
- **Lead with the five-minute pitch** in the behavioral round (Exercise 1); it steers the deep-dive toward your strengths.
- **Don't bluff.** At your knowledge edge, "I didn't go deeper than X; here's how I'd find out" is a pass; confident wrong is the fail (Lecture 1 §2).
- **Grade honestly afterward** with Exercise 3. The overrated round is a finding to fix, not hide.
